            Dual-Class Share Arbitrage
            

              
Stat-arb basket across 6–10 dual-class pairs (GOOGL/GOOG, UAA/UA, and others).             Monte Carlo Option Pricer
            

              
European call/put pricer in Java built on geometric Brownian motion. Simulates 1M+ paths in ~100 ms and lands within 0.01% of the Black–Scholes closed form. Box–Muller transform for normal draws, 95% confidence intervals on every estimate, and put–call parity as a self-check. O(N) time, O(1) space, driven from an interactive CLI.

              

                

                

                

              

              

                View GitHub
              

            

          


          

            UFC Fight-Outcome Predictor
            

              
Random Forest trained and backtested on ~12 years of UFC data. Features include takedown and strike accuracy, takedown defense, age, resume strength, and activity. Outputs a per-fighter win probability, a confidence score, and the most likely method of victory (decision, TKO/KO, or submission). Retrains after every card.
